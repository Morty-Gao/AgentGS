\section{AgentGS}
\label{sec:method}
AgentGS extends a tool-only agent with hierarchical strategy knowledge, Skill-guided plan composition, and automatic completion (Figures~\ref{fig:overview} and~\ref{fig:pipeline}). Composition provides a task-specific starting plan; completion recovers missing connections before the plan is mapped to executable tools.
