\begin{abstract}
Geoscientific analyses require multiple tools to exchange data and intermediate results. Planning around individual tools ties workflows to specific implementations, while reusing entire strategies limits the combination of local operations across tasks. We introduce AgentGS, an agent that composes analytical workflows from reusable operations before selecting their implementations. A hypergraph organizes strategy knowledge, allowing the agent to combine operations across strategies while preserving their prerequisites and usage constraints. When the initial workflow is incomplete, AgentGS searches for compatible operations to connect its fragments. The resulting workflow guides tool selection, intermediate-result propagation, and recovery from execution failures. We also introduce GeoToolEval, a benchmark spanning diverse geoscientific research themes that evaluates final answers alongside tool workflows. Experiments show that AgentGS outperforms existing methods in answer quality and tool-workflow agreement, demonstrating the effectiveness of structured composition and completion for geoscientific tool orchestration.
\end{abstract}
