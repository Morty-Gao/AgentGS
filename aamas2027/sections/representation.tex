\subsection{Hierarchical Organization of Skills and Tools}
\label{sec:representation}
Individual tools provide little guidance about their joint use, while complete Skills may contain operations that a new task does not need. AgentGS exposes the internal operations of reusable Skills, combining strategy-level guidance with the flexibility to compose local operations across strategies.

Each Skill specifies an objective, typed inputs and outputs, and dependencies between operations. A shared operation, termed a \emph{Capability}, maps to one or more implementing tools. We represent this organization as a hypergraph with operations as nodes and Skills as hyperedges. Shared operations connect strategies, while declared dependencies govern their composition.

Reuse preserves the prerequisites and tool constraints of the source Skill. Two strategies can therefore share an operation while requiring different preprocessing steps. Operations on different input objects remain separate, retaining the identity of the result consumed downstream. Appendix~\ref{app:method-details} gives the formal representation.
