\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
AgentGS separates strategies, operations, and implementations to compose local capabilities across Skills. Hypergraph completion uses dependencies with provenance and type/tool constraints; grounding, artifact binding, and local recovery yield executable calls. Verified compositions can form candidate Skills for reuse. AgentGS's main FAS exceeds the evaluated baselines, and configuration comparisons show answer and process-score differences. A1's partial control, A2's inactive ablation, A5's partial ranking intervention, and metrics drawn from different materials prevent interpreting all differences as independent causal module contributions. Scientific correctness additionally requires execution traces.

Coverage depends on registered knowledge. Structural and type checks do not establish task necessity, and recovery is limited to the existing plan. Candidate Skills do not expand underlying capability/tool coverage; their effects on later performance and planning cost remain untested. Future work will integrate task semantics and feedback into completion, assess Skill reuse across tasks, and test unseen combinations and tool changes.
