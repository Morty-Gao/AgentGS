\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
AgentGS separates task strategies, shared operations, and tool implementations to support local capability composition across Skills. Hypergraph completion inserts missing intermediate operations using dependencies with provenance and type/tool constraints. Grounding, artifact binding, and local recovery translate capability plans into actual invocations. GeoToolEval results and ablations support the contributions of hierarchical planning, predecessor expansion, completion, and recovery, particularly for multistep tasks.

Coverage remains limited by registered knowledge. Structural and type checks alone cannot establish whether an inserted operation is necessary for a task's objective, and recovery is confined to the existing plan. Candidate Skills preserve compositions of existing capabilities without expanding underlying capability or tool coverage. Their effects on subsequent performance and planning cost have not been independently evaluated. Future work will combine task semantics and execution feedback to improve completion and plan revision, evaluate Skill reuse across tasks, and test unseen capability combinations and tool changes.
